### Challenge: College Canteen Makeover

## Scenario

You've been given **₹10,000** to improve your college canteen for **one week**. Your goal is to design a plan that keeps students happy, keeps the canteen running smoothly, and doesn't run out of money or food.

## Your Task

Using the ₹10,000 budget, decide:

1. **Menu** — What food and drinks will you offer? (e.g., snacks, meals, beverages)
2. **Pricing** — What will you charge for each item?
3. **Quantities** — How much of each item will you stock/prepare, and how will this change across the week (weekday vs. weekend, morning vs. lunch rush, etc.)?
4. **Demand Management** — How will you handle:
   - Peak hours (long queues, rush periods)
   - Running out of popular items
   - Leftover/unsold food (waste reduction)
   - Student budget constraints (many students may prefer cheaper options)

## Constraints

- Total spending across the week must not exceed **₹10,000**.
- The plan must cover **7 days** of operation.
- Assume the canteen serves roughly **150–300 students per day** (you may adjust and justify a different number).
- You must account for ingredient/food cost, not just selling price.

## Deliverables

Present your plan with:

1. **Budget Breakdown** — A table showing what you spent the ₹10,000 on (ingredients, supplies, etc.)
2. **Menu & Pricing Table** — Item name, cost to make, selling price, expected daily quantity
3. **Daily Demand Strategy** — A short explanation of how you'll manage rush hours, shortages, and leftovers
4. **Profit/Loss Estimate** — Rough calculation of expected revenue vs. cost for the week
5. **Key Assumptions** — Any assumptions you made about student numbers, prices, or preferences

## Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | What We're Looking For |
|---|---|
| **Budget Discipline** | Stays within ₹10,000, realistic pricing |
| **Menu Sense** | Practical, appealing, achievable with given budget |
| **Demand Handling** | Thoughtful strategy for rush hours & shortages |
| **Math & Reasoning** | Clear, logical numbers that add up |
| **Creativity** | Original ideas (combos, discounts, rotating menu, etc.) |

## Bonus Challenge (Optional)

Propose **one creative idea** to increase revenue or reduce waste without increasing the budget — e.g., a loyalty card, a "lunch combo" deal, pre-ordering system, or a daily special.
